SYNELA™ · Textile

Integrated Surface Finishing Agents for Premium Textile Processing

SYNELA™ for textile delivers a complete portfolio — shade enhancers, silicone softeners, print clarity enhancers, plisse agents, general printing binders, and specialty effect binders — engineered for surface performance and consistent field results.

Each formulation is designed to meet the specific demands of polyester, cotton, and blended substrates, with controlled pH and solid content for stable processing.

Company Introduction

Founded in 1993 and based in South Korea, HANSONG INDUSTRY CO., LTD. is a specialized B2B manufacturer of high-quality chemical preparations for premium textile finishing. With three decades of formulation expertise, we partner with textile manufacturers worldwide to deliver consistent, field-proven solutions.

Through continuous research and development — expressed through our SYNELA™ brand — we deliver innovative formulations that meet the evolving needs of premium textile surface processing.
