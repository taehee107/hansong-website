Company Introduction

Founded in 1993 and based in South Korea, HANSONG INDUSTRY CO., LTD. is a specialized B2B manufacturer of high-quality chemical preparations for textile finishing. With three decades of formulation expertise, we partner with manufacturers worldwide to deliver consistent, field-proven solutions.

Through continuous research and development — expressed through our SYNELA™ brand — we deliver innovative formulations that meet the evolving needs of premium textile surface processing.
