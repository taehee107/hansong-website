Polyester deepening agent — BLD series

A fully cationic deepening agent system for deep, tone-controlled black on polyester — for Middle East abaya and India / Southeast Asia fabrics. Two process lines: Formal Black (2-step: acrylic tone build, then silicone deepening) and Jet Black (1-step silicone).

How it works: the coating forms a fine micro-rough surface that scatters less light back to the eye, and a film with a lower refractive index than polyester that cuts surface reflection. Less reflected white light means a deeper, richer black.

Company introduction

Founded in 1993 and based in South Korea, HANSONG INDUSTRY CO., LTD. is a specialized B2B manufacturer of high-quality chemical preparations for textile finishing. With three decades of formulation expertise, we partner with manufacturers worldwide to deliver consistent, field-proven solutions.

Through continuous research and development — expressed through our SYNELA™ brand — we deliver innovative formulations that meet the evolving needs of premium textile surface processing.
